My Story

Author: Arsam Jabbarzadeh | Date: 1403/6/1 |

From the First Line of Code to International Medals

Everything started with a simple question: “How do these systems and websites work behind the scenes?” Many people may think that reaching global competitions and implementing complex web and artificial intelligence architectures takes years; however, my experience showed me that with passion, daily focus, and the courage to face errors (Bugs), the learning journey can gain incredible momentum.

Starting the Adventure with Design and Coding My journey began by learning the standard tools of the software world. I quickly realized that a good project should not only look beautiful, but also work flawlessly from an engineering and logical perspective. Working with development environments such as VS Code, managing and controlling versions with Git and GitHub, working with databases in DBeaver, and simulating servers through XAMPP became a regular part of my daily routine. Designing clean user interfaces in Figma and turning them into optimized front-end code taught me how to transform a raw idea from zero to a tangible digital product. Crossing Boundaries: Competitions and Real-World Challenges The biggest leap happened when the projects went beyond simple exercises and entered the competitive arena: Istanbul Tourism Project: A major challenge in data management and user interaction that allowed us to stand out and achieve a ranking in Turkey’s web competitions. SkyGate Project: An innovative platform implemented according to the strictest international web and software architecture standards, which impressed the judges of the Web Inovus global competition in the United States and brought us a gold medal. Innovation and Global Invention: Winning the gold medal from the International Federation of Inventors’ Associations (IFIA) in Geneva, Switzerland, confirmed that creativity and problem-solving know no geographical or age boundaries. Coordinated teamwork and solving technical challenges under competition pressure taught me that failure is part of coding; every error is simply guidance to help you write better code. The Road Ahead: Diving Deeper into Artificial Intelligence (AI) The web world is changing at an astonishing pace, and I believe tomorrow’s websites and software will not survive without artificial intelligence. My current focus is learning algorithms and implementing intelligent tools. I am preparing for more advanced AI competitions and projects so I can build products that learn, think, and make people’s lives easier. My message to anyone at the beginning of their learning journey: Don’t wait for the ideal opportunity; turn on your system, write your first line of code, and don’t be afraid of bugs. The greatest achievements are hidden right after the hardest challenges.
